Melbourne-based Quality Engineer with 10+ years' experience in quality assurance (QA) and software testing across digital banking, payments, mobile and web platforms, spanning ANZ, ANZ Plus, Cash App and Crown Resorts. Specialises in mobile quality engineering and test automation (iOS/Android), API testing, test strategy and defect management within complex financial and Agile environments. Builds practical automation and AI-assisted tooling. Known for identifying risk early, mentoring test analysts, and helping squads ship secure, reliable, customer-focused releases.
